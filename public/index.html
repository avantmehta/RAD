❓ How Heartfood works
  • 📍 Tell us your situation — tap quick buttons for how you travel, your budget, and your schedule, or just type it in your own words.
  • ⏳ We rank what's actually reachable — pantries, mobile markets, and SNAP grocers, sorted by real travel time for your mode of transport, not just distance on a map.
  • ✅ We flag what's likely open — so you're not sent somewhere that's actually closed when you get there.
  • 🎯 You can fine-tune it — food type, payment accepted, and more, all with a few taps.
