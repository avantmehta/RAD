# FoodReach CT: a trip that fits your day

## Problem and evidence

A nearby pantry may still be unusable if the resident cannot arrive before intake ends, return before a commitment, or walk the distance required. Existing tools already help discover resources; do not claim this is the world's first food locator. Our differentiator is transparent round-trip feasibility and a small-change alternative when the resident's window does not fit.

Evidence collected during this build:

- Hands On Hartford's pantry page lists 11:30 a.m. as its morning cutoff; its assistance page lists noon. Source conflict matters near closing. https://handsonhartford.org/programs/community-pantry/ and https://handsonhartford.org/assistance/
- Bloomfield's current page says evening shopping is suspended. Reusing an older flyer could send a resident to a closed distribution. https://www.bloomfieldct.gov/425/Food-Resources
- East Hartford has ordinal monthly distribution dates, not a weekly Thursday service. https://www.easthartfordct.gov/FoodPantry
- Newington requires arrangements for monthly pickup; opening hours alone do not establish access. https://www.newingtonct.gov/2281/Food-Bank

## Persona — illustrative, not a research participant

A resident near Union Station can use the bus, has a limited walking budget and must return before another commitment. The prototype uses no invented interview quotes or impact statistics.

## Two-minute demo

1. Select Union Station, bus, September 30 2026, leave 10:30, return 14:00, 40 minutes total walking, 20-minute pickup.
2. Show MANNA's sourced candidate and separately calculated return journey. Open the details and point out the conflicting morning cutoff.
3. Change the return deadline to 10:35. Show that the trip is no longer offered.
4. Show a calculated alternative time/date in My Food Window. Explain that the resident chooses whether changing their schedule is possible.
5. Download the trip plan and open the source link.

Use the natural-language entrance only after API quota is resolved and the integration passes. Do not present the fallback fields as an AI response.

## Five slides

1. Nearby is not always reachable: resident scenario.
2. Evidence: schedule conflicts, appointments and monthly recurrence.
3. Live product: constraints, round trip and alternatives.
4. Trust: sourced schedules, explicit estimates, unknown stock, human review.
5. Pilot: partner with a caseworker/pantry; measure actionable plans and prevented unsuccessful journeys.

## Honest boundary

Fifteen sourced resources and three origins, direct buses only. Walking and car times are estimates. No stock prediction, phone confirmation or measured social impact yet. A compelling prototype demonstrates the mechanism without overstating deployment readiness.
